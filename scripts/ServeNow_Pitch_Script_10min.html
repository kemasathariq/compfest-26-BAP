ServeNow 2.0 — the 10-minute pitch script

Word-for-word delivery script for the ServeNow × BAP deck, slide by slide, in the bilingual Indonesian–English register most judge panels actually hear in the room — full Indonesian for warmth and framing, English for the technical spine and every number.

Runtime ≈10:00–10:15 Pace 145–155 wpm Slides 1–11 live, 12–16 reserve Presenters 3

§1 How this script is built

Bilingual by design

Narrative, transitions and rapport-building lines run in Indonesian — that's what a live room actually relaxes into. Every metric, architecture term and proper noun (SLA, n8n, Qdrant RAG, FTE) stays in English, matched exactly to the deck's own wording so nothing sounds translated.

Say it, don't read it

This is a memorization aid, not a teleprompter script. Read each slide's block twice, then paraphrase in your own words — the beat notes tell you what has to land, not which syllable to hit.

Timing is a target, not a cage

Each slide carries a time budget at 145–155 words/minute. Rehearse against a stopwatch twice; then trust your own rhythm over the clock in the room.

Slides 12–16 are reserve

References and the four appendix slides (SOP, implementation plan, mockup, financial detail) exist for Q&A, not the timed run — click through fast or skip, and pull from them only if a judge asks.

SLIDE 2 / 11

Executive Summary

Restructuring ServeNow's Governance and Escalation Architecture

0:25–1:35

Jadi, apa masalahnya? ServeNow saat ini beroperasi without a governance model. 78 staff, effectively reporting ke tiga direktur, yang masing-masing memegang 5 to 8 roles sekaligus. Throughput perusahaan ini sepenuhnya bottlenecked oleh director bandwidth.

Kita identifikasi tiga masalah inti: no ownership state — nobody knows who owns a conversation. No accountability — unclaimed work just sits there. Dan cost & visibility leak — koordinasi internal bocor ke channel WhatsApp yang metered.

Maka pertanyaan kuncinya: [slow down here, this is the thesis] how can ServeNow relocate capability, decision rights, and customer trust dari tiga founder ke sebuah sistem yang accountable — sambil mendorong SLA compliance ke 96%?

Jawaban kami, tiga pillar: Governance & Escalation Architecture, Single-Number GenAI Omnichannel, dan AI-Qualified Lead Engine & Expansion. Dan hasilnya sudah terukur — SLA +18 points, churn −70%, employees-per-customer −24%, implementation time −55%, revenue outside Jabodetabek >40%.

Delivery Land each impact number as its own beat — a half-second gap between them, not a run-on list. This is the slide judges write numbers down from.

SLIDE 3 / 11

Background Analysis — Issue Tree

Nine root causes, one conclusion: ownership state and escalation instruments are missing

1:35–2:35

Kenapa masalah ini bisa terjadi? Kami breakdown jadi nine root causes, tiga kategori, fully MECE — Governance & Escalation, Product & Cost Architecture, dan Sales & Customer Trust.

Contohnya: literally nothing records who owns a chat. SLA-nya satu angka flat untuk semua kategori, jadi kerjaan bisa nganggur sampai ada yang notice manual. Koordinasi internal bocor ke WhatsApp channel yang metered — bayar per anggota tim. Dan revenue masih menempel di relasi pribadi founder — itu yang block delegation dan ekspansi geografis.

[this is the pivot line — say it directly to the panel] Tapi insight kuncinya di sini: ketiga pillar ini bukan parallel workstream. Governance adalah enabling layer — dia yang menciptakan decision capacity dan named ownership yang dibutuhkan Product dan Sales untuk bisa berjalan sama sekali. Fix ownership first — baru yang lain mengikuti.

Delivery The pivot line ("Governance is the enabling layer") is your whole deck's logic in one sentence — make eye contact across the panel while saying it.

SLIDE 4 / 11

Pillar I — Governance & Escalation Architecture

An atomic claim protocol and a four-layer operating model

2:35–4:10

Masuk ke Pillar satu: Governance & Escalation Architecture. Ini jantungnya seluruh transformasi.

How work moves — [point to the flow diagram, left to right] pesan pelanggan masuk, di-route lewat mode gate, lalu first qualified owner claim, jadi accountable owner. Simpel, tapi powerful — claim-nya atomic, jadi tidak ada dua orang yang bisa klaim percakapan yang sama.

Who owns it — target operating model kami: 3 Directors fokus di strategy dan exceptions saja. Di bawahnya, 6 Functional Owners baru yang own outcomes within defined boundaries. Team Leads coordinate execution. Dan 78 staff yang sudah ada, jumlahnya tetap sama, tapi sekarang punya named manager yang jelas.

Bounded decision rights — keputusan rutin selesai di level functional owner. Kalau ada SLA risk, team lead yang coordinates recovery. Baru kalau sudah exception atau breach, director turun tangan — intervenes only when escalated.

Hasilnya? Routine decisions move down, exceptions move up, dan every handoff stays auditable. Ini bukan sekadar org chart baru — ini sistem yang punya memory.

Delivery Slowest, most deliberate slide in the deck — this is the load-bearing pillar. Use the diagram as your anchor: point, don't just talk over it.
~225 words

SLIDE 5 / 11

Pillar II — Single-Number GenAI Omnichannel

One WhatsApp number, an AI classifier, and zero internal coordination cost

4:10–5:30

Pillar dua: Single-Number GenAI Omnichannel. Apa itu? Satu nomor WhatsApp. Bot handle FAQ dan langsung handoff ke shared agent console — instan.

Cara kerjanya, lima langkah: inbound query masuk di satu nomor. n8n router dan LLM classify intent-nya — FAQ, lead, atau complaint. Qdrant RAG langsung resolve FAQ tanpa perlu agent. Kalau complex lead atau complaint, trigger handoff ke web console. Dan agent pertama yang claim, dia yang handle — semua event ter-log untuk SLA audit.

Kenapa ini penting? Kita eliminate WhatsApp group yang mahal itu — obrolan internal pindah ke channel gratis. Qdrant vector search resolve FAQ tanpa manusia sama sekali. Pelanggan tetap dapat satu continuous thread, walau di belakang layar, lead-nya di-broadcast ke semua agent — gratis. Dan direct-SQL antara n8n dan Postgres, tanpa REST layer, supaya kita hit target rollout 6 to 10 weeks.

Delivery "Direct-SQL, no REST layer" reads as a throwaway line but it's the technical justification for the 6–10 week claim — don't rush past it.
~185 words

SLIDE 6 / 11

Pillar III — AI-Qualified Lead Engine & Expansion

AI hunts, humans harvest — every account gets a named owner

5:30–6:55

Pillar tiga: AI-Qualified Lead Engine & Expansion. Filosofinya simpel — AI does the hunting, humans do the harvesting.

AI mengualifikasi inbound interest 24 jam nonstop — high intent versus unclear intent. Qualified leads masuk ke pipeline dashboard milik Head of Sales, target-nya 3× coverage. Account executive baru masuk untuk demo, proposal, negotiate, close. Dan setiap akun dapat named institutional owner — bukan lagi founder.

Kenapa ini penting? Sekarang CEO masih negosiasi pricing secara personal untuk semua deal. Dengan sistem ini, Head of Sales yang absorb routine deals dalam band diskon −15% — kapasitas founder pun terbebas. Named ownership juga yang mulai membangun institutional trust — kunci untuk ekspansi ke luar Jabodetabek.

Go-to-market-nya: cloud-only delivery, jadi tidak perlu kantor fisik per region. B2B digital marketing lewat LinkedIn, target regional corporates. Dan positioning kami jelas — AI Omnichannel that eliminates WhatsApp Group Handoff cost, langsung berhadapan dengan kompetitor global yang mahal dan rigid, atau lokal yang agresif tapi kurang AI-native.

Delivery "AI hunts, humans harvest" is the quotable line — say it slightly slower and let it be the thing judges remember about Pillar III.
~205 words

SLIDE 7 / 11

Risk & Mitigations

Every pillar carries a defined trigger and mitigation protocol

6:55–7:45

Tentu, transformasi sebesar ini punya risiko. Kami map setiap pillar ke potential risk dan mitigation-nya masing-masing.

Yang paling critical: enam Functional Owner baru bisa underperform di dua kuartal pertama, dan founder mungkin sulit genuinely melepas otoritas setelah tiga tahun memegang kendali langsung. Mitigasinya? Written decision thresholds, Shadow-to-Solo Gate, dan Decision Rights Matrix yang binding — bukan sekadar advisory.

Untuk risiko teknis — misalnya bot masih reply setelah chat di-claim — kami hard-gate di level workflow, tested explicitly sebelum production. Intinya: effective risk mitigation butuh continuous validation, flexible guardrails, dan hybrid approach — bukan perbaikan sekali jalan.

Delivery This slide reassures, it doesn't alarm — keep the tone matter-of-fact. You already have the mitigation for every risk you name, so say the risk plainly.
~130 words

Timeline & KPIs

12 months from planning to full rollout, tracked by KPIs

[count these off on your fingers if it helps the pace] Target KPI kami jelas: SLA attainment minimal 96%, churn di bawah 5%, employees-per-customer maksimal 1.41, implementation time 6 to 10 weeks, net margin 15 to 20%, dan revenue menembus Rp45 billion — dengan 40% darinya dari luar Jabodetabek.

Financial Projection — From Governance to Financial Impact

Two governance-owned benefits, valued conservatively

8:35–9:20

Sekarang, angka-angkanya. Dari governance ini saja, ada dua benefit utama. Pertama, capacity efficiency — kami recover sekitar 22 FTE-equivalent capacity, senilai Rp3.57 billion per tahun dalam avoided labour cost. Kedua, churn avoidance — governance's bounded share saja, retaining 8.4 customers, senilai Rp1.896 billion per tahun.

Total gross annual benefit di tahun ketiga: Rp5.47 billion. [pause — let the honesty land] Dan yang penting, angka ini belum termasuk revenue growth ke Rp45 billion, faster implementation, atau expansion revenue. Itu murni milik Product dan Sales. Ini betul-betul governance-owned share saja — conservative by design.

Delivery "Conservative by design" is a credibility line, not filler — it's the thing that stops a judge asking "did you inflate this?" before they ask it.

Financial Projection — Investment Case & Return

Rp384M in, Rp3.73B/yr out, 19-month payback
